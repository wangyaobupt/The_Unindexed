最近读了Mark Twain的短篇MY LATE SENATORIAL SECRETARYSHIP（1867），发现150年前的故事居然与今天的Agent出奇地神似。

这个故事的人物是一位Senator和他的秘书，Senator口述意图，然后委托秘书替他回复相关信件。全文讲了四封信，咱们得一封一封看。

## 第一封信

Senator 口述意图

> "I gave you a letter from certain of my constituents in the State of Nevada, asking the establishment of a post-office at Baldwin's Ranch, and told you to answer it, as ingeniously as you could, with arguments which should persuade them that there was no real necessity for an office at that place."

秘书写出的邮件

> 'WASHINGTON, Nov. 24
>
> 'Messrs. Smith, Jones, and others.
>
> 'GENTLEMEN: What the mischief do you suppose you want with a post-office at Baldwin's Ranch? It would not do you any good. If any letters came there, you couldn't read them, you know; and, besides, such letters as ought to pass through, with money in them, for other localities, would not be likely to get through, you must perceive at once; and that would make trouble for us all. No, don't bother about a post-office in your camp. I have your best interests at heart, and feel that it would only be an ornamental folly. What you want is a nice jail, you know--a nice, substantial jail and a free school. These will be a lasting benefit to you. These will make you really contented and happy. I will move in the matter at once.
>
> 'Very truly, etc.,<br>Mark Twain,<br>'For James W. N------, U. S. Senator.'

背景

1. 1867年的美国，识字率并不高，特别是在Baldwin's Ranch这类西部农场
2. 当时的邮局不只是一个邮箱，而是对外联系的管道，信件、汇款、商业通信、法律地址都得依赖邮局。一个农场如果能建立邮局，表示联邦政府已经对当地有了足够认可

点评

1. 秘书相当忠实地实现了Senator的意图，且逻辑完备
   1. “If any letters came there, you couldn't read them, you know”：清楚明白地说明了为啥你们不需要一个邮局，你们没几个识字的
   2. 秘书还即兴发挥了一段，你们不但不识字，而且还会偷盗邮件里面的钱：“such letters as ought to pass through, with money in them, for other localities, would not be likely to get through……”
   3. 有了前面两点，秘书在逻辑上不但实现了“说服你用不着什么”，而且，像聊天机器人一样，给出了“你实际需要什么”：substantial jail and a free school. 逻辑完备。
2. Senator满意吗？Senator自己说“Those people say they will hang me, if I ever enter that district again; and I am perfectly satisfied they will, too.”
3. 是谁的问题？Senator内心未必不是和秘书想的一样，但是，“心里可以这么想，话不能这么说”。为什么“话不能这么说”，因为社会运行有其约束，一个参议员的形象决定了在纸面上要表现出温良恭俭让。

## 第二封信

Senator 口述意图

> Now, here is another specimen. I gave you a petition from certain gentlemen of Nevada, praying that I would get a bill through Congress incorporating the Methodist Episcopal Church of the State of Nevada. I told you to say, in reply, that the creation of such a law came more properly within the province of the state legislature; and to endeavor to show them that, in the present feebleness of the religious element in that new commonwealth, the expediency of incorporating the church was questionable. What did you write?

秘书的邮件

> "'WASHINGTON, Nov. 24.
>
> "'Rev. John Halifax and others.
>
> "'GENTLEMEN: You will have to go to the state legislature about that speculation of yours--Congress don't know anything about religion. But don't you hurry to go there, either; because this thing you propose to do out in that new country isn't expedient--in fact, it is ridiculous. Your religious people there are too feeble, in intellect, in morality, in piety in everything, pretty much. You had better drop this--you can't make it work. You can't issue stock on an incorporation like that--or if you could, it would only keep you in trouble all the time. The other denominations would abuse it, and "bear" it, and "sell it short," and break it down. They would do with it just as they would with one of your silver-mines out there--they would try to make all the world believe it was "wildcat." You ought not to do anything that is calculated to bring a sacred thing into disrepute. You ought to be ashamed of yourselves that is what I think about it. You close your petition with the words: "And we will ever pray." I think you had better you need to do it.
>
> "'Very truly, etc.,<br>"'MARK TWAIN,<br>"'For James W. N-----, U. S. Senator.'

背景注释

1. 对方请求incorporating the Methodist Episcopal Church 不是建立商业机构，而是建立一个类似于法人的宗教机构，church。
2. 1860年代，美国的宗教机构和政府早已分离，联邦政府不介入此类议题
3. 1860年代的Nevada充斥着矿产投机，speculation，发行股票等等交易与今天的硅谷无异。

点评

1. “政教分离”、“联邦政府不干预”等抽象的大词，被秘书用大白话写出来了：Congress don't know anything about religion. 你说他写错了吗，似乎也挑不出错误。
2. 再一次自我发挥：在写出当下不方便做这件事之后，居然补了一句 in fact, it is ridiculous。于是，后面的token就一发不可收拾。不但引用了Senator口头说过的feeble，而且居然“联想”到了设立公司和上市：
   1. 你注定推出不了：You can't issue stock on an incorporation like that
   2. 其他宗教捣乱，而且是做空你 The other denominations would abuse it, and "bear" it, and "sell it short," and break it down.
   3. 捣乱的人会让世界觉得你是骗子，they would try to make all the world believe it was "wildcat." wildcat这个词在今天的投资圈子里几乎相当于“空气项目”
3. 秘书不是不懂讽刺，最后一句"And we will ever pray." I think you had better you need to do it. 把领导口中的‘incorporating the church was questionable’ 直白无误地告诉了申请人，没戏了。

## 第三封信

Senator 口述意图

> some evil instinct prompted me to hand you this memorial from the grave company of elders composing the board of aldermen of the city of San Francisco, to try your hand upon--a memorial praying that the city's right to the water-lots upon the city front might be established by law of Congress. I told you this was a dangerous matter to move in. I told you to write a non-committal letter to the aldermen--an ambiguous letter--a letter that should avoid, as far as possible, all real consideration and discussion of the water-lot question.

秘书的邮件

> 'WASHINGTON, Nov. 27
>
> 'The Honorable Board of Aldermen, etc.
>
> 'GENTLEMEN: George Washington, the revered Father of his Country, is dead. His long and brilliant career is closed, alas! forever. He was greatly respected in this section of the country, and his untimely decease cast a gloom over the whole community. He died on the 14th day of December, 1799. He passed peacefully away from the scene of his honors and his great achievements, the most lamented hero and the best beloved that ever earth hath yielded unto Death. At such a time as this, you speak of water-lots! what a lot was his!
>
> 'What is fame! Fame is an accident. Sir Isaac Newton discovered an apple falling to the ground--a trivial discovery, truly, and one which a million men had made before him--but his parents were influential, and so they tortured that small circumstance into something wonderful, and, lo! the simple world took up the shout and, in almost the twinkling of an eye, that man was famous. Treasure these thoughts.
>
> 'Poesy, sweet poesy, who shall estimate what the world owes to thee!
>
> "Mary had a little lamb, its fleece was white as snow-- And everywhere that Mary went, the lamb was sure to go."
>
> "Jack and Gill went up the hill<br>To draw a pail of water;<br>Jack fell down and broke his crown,<br>And Gill came tumbling after."
>
> 'For simplicity, elegance of diction, and freedom from immoral tendencies, I regard those two poems in the light of gems. They are suited to all grades of intelligence, to every sphere of life --to the field, to the nursery, to the guild. Especially should no Board of Aldermen be without them.
>
> 'Venerable fossils! write again. Nothing improves one so much as friendly correspondence. Write again--and if there is anything in this memorial of yours that refers to anything in particular, do not be backward about explaining it. We shall always be happy to hear you chirp.
>
> 'Very truly, etc.,<br>"'MARK TWAIN,<br>'For James W. N-----, U. S. Senator.'

背景注释

1. San Francisco “water-lots”不是供水问题，是产权问题。在淘金热时代，SF的填海造地产生了大量新的土地，这些就是water-lot，问题是这些新土地属于谁？所以Senator才会说 this was a dangerous matter to move in。

点评

1. 领导说“a letter that should avoid, as far as possible,”，于是秘书起手就是从Washington的去世说起，足够远，果然是as far as possible。为了更远，下一段是牛顿，后面的典故不一而足。
2. 你说他有没有完成领导意图呢？字面意义上，完成得相当好。但领导居然极不满意。

## 第四封信

Senator 口述意图

> I had my misgivings when I gave you the letter from Humboldt, asking that the post route from Indian Gulch to Shakespeare Gap and intermediate points be changed partly to the old Mormon trail. But I told you it was a delicate question, and warned you to deal with it deftly--to answer it dubiously, and leave them a little in the dark.

秘书的邮件

> "'WASHINGTON, Nov. 30.
>
> "'Messrs. Perkins, Wagner, et al.
>
> "'GENTLEMEN: It is a delicate question about this Indian trail, but, handled with proper deftness and dubiousness, I doubt not we shall succeed in some measure or otherwise, because the place where the route leaves the Lassen Meadows, over beyond where those two Shawnee chiefs, Dilapidated Vengeance and Biter-of-the-Clouds, were scalped last winter, this being the favorite direction to some, but others preferring something else in consequence of things, the Mormon trail leaving Mosby's at three in the morning, and passing through Jawbone Flat to Blucher, and then down by Jug-Handle, the road passing to the right of it, and naturally leaving it on the right, too, and Dawson's on the left of the trail where it passes to the left of said Dawson's and onward thence to Tomahawk, thus making the route cheaper, easier of access to all who can get at it, and compassing all the desirable objects so considered by others, and, therefore, conferring the most good upon the greatest number, and, consequently, I am encouraged to hope we shall. However, I shall be ready, and happy, to afford you still further information upon the subject, from time to time, as you may desire it and the Post-office Department be enabled to furnish it to me.
>
> "'Very truly, etc.,<br>"'MARK TWAIN,
>
> "'For James W. N-----, U. S. Senator.'

点评

1. 和第一封信一样，邮路问题不是技术问题，是利益博弈问题。领导口中的to answer it dubiously, and leave them a little in the dark 恰恰是资源分配者常用的策略，给你虚假的可能性，但不承诺任何事
2. 秘书开门见山地说这是一个棘手的问题 a delicate question，算是忠实引用了领导意图。然后，开始‘报菜名’，讲了一大堆地名之后，开始说常识：‘this being the favorite direction to some, but others preferring something else’
3. 在一般人的逻辑里，你说了不可能让所有人都满意，接下来，要么把这一方的利益给那一方，要么把那一方的利益给这一方。但领导说了 leave them a little in the dark，于是秘书开创了第三条路，继续什么都没说地写下去，开始讲地理课：the Mormon trail leaving …
4. 讲完地理课，再次回到无人能反驳的常识，“我们”希望能综合考虑绝大多数人的最大利益：conferring the most good upon the greatest number, and, consequently, I am encouraged to hope we shall.
5. 最后，秘书发挥了两句，表现出Senator对来函者的关心：I shall be ready, and happy, to afford you still further information upon the subject, from time to time, as you may desire it and the Post-office Department be enabled to furnish it to me.

## 这位秘书和LLM/Agent如此相似

用今天的眼光看这四封信，我可以写出如下Result Table

| Benchmark Metric | Result |
| --- | --- |
| 任务完成度 | 100% |
| 忠实于意图 | YES |
| 自主完成，中途不向用户提问 | YES |

但很显然，Senator不满意，150年来任何一个读者读下来也不会觉得秘书完成了他自己的工作。站在“社会”的框架下面来看，这四封信的问题在于人类社会沟通包含了很多“维度”，你不能搞成“单维度优化问题”。

1. 秘书正确理解了领导的意图，但是不知道哪些“适合”说出来 Direct action detached from social state
2. 秘书选错了语言的框架，或者叫做本体 Reasoning detached from correct ontology
3. 秘书固然做到了离题万里，避而不答，但是忘记了“基本约束”，作为一封回信，总要与原本问题看起来相关 Instruction-following detached from intention
4. 秘书写出了一段自己都不一定知道对错的地理路径，确实将来信者置于不清楚的状态 Fluent language detached from information

这像不像今天的LLM和Agent，在技术社区眼里，

回信 = NLU + reasoning + NLG

每一件事都早已被LLM“解决”了

然而，假使把LLM或者加上harness的LLM Agent放在秘书这个位置，谁又敢说不会写出马克吐温笔下的荒诞邮件呢？

不过，我得替秘书和LLM辩解一句，针对第四封信的场景，在LLM之前，很多大公司的邮件也是如此，conferring the most good upon the greatest number几乎是一种标志性句式。你不能说写出这样的邮件是LLM的问题，确实有人这样写邮件。

## 关键在于它会产生后果

你生成的不只是一段文本，而是对真实世界的干预行为（intervention），会对世界产生影响，你（或者其他人）需要对影响承担后果。马克吐温笔下的秘书，和早期LLM Chatbot一样，不理解这个后果。即便是今天装备了最前沿推理能力的Agent，恐怕也不一定能替这位Senator完整评估后果。

你可能会说，干预不就是action吗？那么这不就是RL的场景化，只要给我足够的后训练数据，我总能让模型学会某个action可能的后果是什么，这些问题不都解决了吗？

恐怕没这么简单。

当你在RL数据中引导模型“简单直接不要绕圈子”，得到的就是“我直接用最直白、最简单、最朴实、最坦然、最真实、最实际、最利落、最干脆、最不绕弯、最不兜圈子、最不拐弯抹角的话告诉你……”，然后，你的Agent会不会在写第一封信的时候直接说“你识字吗，要邮局干什么？”

你当然可以In Context Learning，在每个任务前面描述我想要生成什么风格的文字。然而，下一个问题接踵而至：你说得出你的需求吗？你在和真人合作中会这么做吗？你凭啥认为你给Agent的context可以代表你的真实需求？

正如[此前一篇文章](https://wangyaobupt.github.io/The_Unindexed/2026/07/12/why-seventy-is-easy-and-ninety-takes-dozens-of-rounds.html)所述，这是一个通信问题。问题是人脑中包含着相当高维度的信息，如果你想用语言一个个全部准确表达出来，可能吗？更不要说，自然语言的编码和理解都是有偏差的，否则为什么我们要发明形式语言描述数学？

## 出路

如果你打算让Agent真正进入能解决此类“真实世界（人类社会）应用”的领域，唯一的出路只能是让模型具备预判结果的能力，有人称之为世界模型。

你没看错，不是机器人，也需要世界模型。

一个写信的秘书需要能够预判“某个语言风格、行文方式、语气语调 对于 收信人 X 意味着什么？产生什么后果”，只有这样，他才能替领导直接寄出邮件，否则，他只能写草稿，寄出邮件的决定权还得要领导拍板。而需要领导拍板就意味着领导自己仍然是瓶颈。就像如果一个代码项目中关键决策还是需要我judge，那么无论agent如何强大，项目的瓶颈还是在我。

这个世界模型不见得要是explicit model。以人作为参考，我确信“每个人头脑中都有世界模型”，但我看不到“张三头脑中的世界模型”。假使我请张三来当我的同事，我只能通过一些场景下他的表现，来推断“他和我是（不是）一路人”，我没法确信他和我看世界的方式是一样的。更常见的场景是，两个合作了十几年无比默契的伙伴，在某一天惊诧于“你怎么是这样想的”，往往到这一刻，我总会想到Dostoyevsky的名言：“我描绘人内心的全部深度。” ‘全部深度’四个字是这位巨匠一生追求而未得的，我们凭什么自大到认为我们很容易解决呢？

或许，有一天LLM会内化世界模型的能力，它不需要外挂一个世界模型，而是通过其权重，具备了在内部评价“如果我做X，世界会发生Y”的推理能力。这个世界模型不需要完美，没有任何人的世界模型与真实世界一模一样，关键问题是要能规避毁灭性风险，随着agent在世界中的经历而动态进化。
